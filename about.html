Our story

Community, sunshine, and a little elbow grease

We're a family owned, locally operated car wash with one goal: bring a bit of brightness to your day, every time you visit.

Since 2024

Rooted in Davis, built by neighbors

Sunny's Car Wash opened its doors in 2024 right on Mace Blvd, and from day one it's been about more than just a clean car. As a family owned, locally operated business, we set out to serve our friends and neighbors with the kind of personal touch you don't get from a big chain.

Whether you're a student squeezing in a wash between classes or a longtime Davis resident running errands, we want every visit to feel a little easier — and a little sunnier — than the one before.

Our passion for shine goes deeper

Quality, sustainability, and a ride that shines as bright as you

Caring for your car shouldn't come at the planet's expense — so we built our wash around doing both well.

💧

Water recycling

Our state-of-the-art system reclaims and reuses water on-site with every single wash.

Solar power

Eco-friendly chemicals

Every soap and sealant we use is chosen to be tough on grime, gentle on Davis.

Not just the exterior

Little extras that keep the whole ride shining

We're not just about exterior dazzle — free vacuums and dash wipes mean the inside gets some love too. Got a bike instead of a car? We've got you covered with a free bike wash and fix-it station.

And if a furry co-pilot rides along, our dog wash station is available to Sunny's Shine Club SPF 50 members at no extra charge.
